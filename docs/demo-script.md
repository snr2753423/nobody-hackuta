Before recording:

## Capture
1. macOS screen recording: press ⌘⇧5, choose *Record Selected Portion*, and turn on the microphone under Options.
2. Record take 1 following the table. Record take 2 immediately as backup.
3. Watch both takes all the way through. Check that:
   - the voice is audible,
   - the skeleton and gesture chips are visible,
   - there's no dead time over 2 seconds.
4. Pick the clean take. Trim only the start and end. Upload it to YouTube (unlisted) and paste the link into Devpost.

Recording needs your camera and voice, so you have to do this yourself.
