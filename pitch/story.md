# Story

## Problem: one concrete scene
Odysseus is trapped in a cave with a one-eyed giant who eats his men two at a time. He can't win by strength: the boulder at the door is too heavy. He wins by **inventing**. He tells the Cyclops his name is "Nobody", so when the blinded giant screams "Nobody is hurting me!", nobody comes. Most games about heroes hand you fixed buttons. Homer's hero survives because he makes up the move nobody expected.

## What we built: one sentence
NOBODY is a webcam game of the Polyphemus episode. Your body is the controller, and you can **forge your own power**: do any movement three times, describe it in plain words, and from then on that movement casts it in combat.

## Why it matters: one sentence
It turns the Odyssey's central idea, cleverness over strength, into a mechanic. Beyond the three built-in moves, the player can invent their own controls mid-fight, running entirely on a laptop webcam with no model training.
